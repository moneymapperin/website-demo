3.How We Use Your Data (Data Collection Scope for the Live Calculator)

The income, expenses, liabilities, and family financial profile data you enter is used specifically to:

  • Calculate your personalized "Financial Fitness Score" across the Income, Expenses, Savings, Protection, and Investment pillars in real time (live calculator);
  • Generate rule-based, algorithmic educational suggestions relevant to your financial profile;
  • Track your progress over time (gamification: XP, levels, streaks) as you improve your score;
  • Personalize in-app content and notifications relevant to your financial goals;
  • Process your subscription billing and manage your account;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and audit obligations;
  • Improve and troubleshoot the Platform (in aggregated/anonymized form wherever possible).

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We do not use your financial profile data for any purpose beyond what is described in this Policy without obtaining your fresh consent.

4.Legal Basis / Consent

  • We process your personal data on the basis of your explicit, informed, and specific consent, obtained through clear affirmative action (e.g., an unchecked consent checkbox that you actively tick) at the time of signup, as required under the DPDPA. We do not use pre-ticked consent boxes.
  • You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to Section 7 (Data Principal Rights) below. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before such withdrawal, and may result in your inability to use certain features of the Platform.

5.Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share limited personal data with:

  • Payment Gateway Partners (RBI-authorized) — to process subscription payments and auto-debit mandates;
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting Providers — with data centers located within India, for secure storage and processing;
  • Analytics Service Providers — for aggregated, where feasible pseudonymized, usage analytics;
  • Regulators/Law Enforcement — where required under applicable Indian law, court order, or governmental request;
  • Professional Advisors — auditors, legal counsel, under confidentiality obligations.

We do not share your data with third parties for their independent marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

6.Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • All personal and financial data is stored on servers located within India, protected using industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews.
  • Passwords are stored in hashed/encrypted form and are never visible to MoneyMapper staff in plain text.
  • We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required under applicable law (e.g., financial/tax record-keeping requirements), after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.
  • Upon account deletion request (see Section 7), we will erase your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except data we are legally required to retain.

7.Your Rights as a Data Principal (Under the DPDPA)

As required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction and Completion: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, and updating of data that is out of date.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You can raise a grievance regarding processing of your personal data — see our Grievance Redressal Policy.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate another individual to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, in the manner prescribed under the DPDPA.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time via account settings or by writing to our Grievance Officer / Data Protection contact.

To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at info@moneymapper.in or to our Grievance Officer at grievance@moneymapper.in. We will respond within the timeline prescribed under the DPDPA and its rules.

You can also submit a permanent data deletion request using our Data Deletion Request form.

8.Children's Data

The Platform is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you believe a child's data has been provided to us, please contact us for immediate deletion.

9.Cookies

Our use of cookies, session tokens, and analytics tracking on the web login dashboard is described in detail in our separate Cookie Policy.

10.Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, MoneyMapper will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the DPDPA and its rules.

11.Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notice, and continued use of the Platform after such notice constitutes acceptance.
