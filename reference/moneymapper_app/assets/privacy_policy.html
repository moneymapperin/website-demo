About MoneyMapper & Privacy Policy

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)

Effective Date: 28 August 2026
Operated by: Monezra Mapper Private Limited ("MoneyMapper", "we", "us", "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyMapper (Monezra Mapper Private Limited) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

1.Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with financial health insights:

  • Personal: Name, email, phone, date of birth, city/state, employment status.
  • Financial: Monthly income, expenses, savings, liabilities/EMIs, insurance and investment details (self-reported by you).
  • Payment: Plan selected, billing history and payment status. Card/UPI details are handled directly by our RBI-authorized payment partner. We never store your full card number or UPI PIN.
  • Usage: Device type, OS, IP address, and feature interactions.
  • Support: Details you share when you contact us.
📌 Important Notice MoneyMapper does not collect your bank credentials or conduct transactions on your behalf.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate your Financial Fitness Score and show your progress.
  • To give you rule-based, educational suggestions.
  • To manage your account and subscription billing.
  • To improve and fix the app (in aggregated form wherever possible).
  • To meet legal, tax, and audit requirements.

We use your data only for these purposes. We ask for fresh consent before using it for anything else.

3.Your Consent

We process your data only with your clear consent, given by actively ticking the consent box. You can withdraw consent anytime. Some features may stop working after withdrawal.

4.Data Security & Storage

  • Stored in India: All data is stored on servers located within India.
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Confidentiality: Your information is never sold or rented to third parties.
  • Access Control: Passwords are stored in hashed form. Only authorized people can access systems.
🔒 🇮🇳 Data Residency Your data is stored securely in India.

5.Who We Share Data With

Only limited data, and only where needed, with:

  • Payment gateway partners (to process payments)
  • Hosting and analytics providers (servers in India)
  • Government or legal authorities, when required by law
  • Auditors and legal advisors, under confidentiality

6.How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. After that, it is deleted or anonymized. If you delete your account, we remove your data, except what we must keep by law.

7.Your Rights

You can:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct wrong or outdated data
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw consent anytime
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance if something is wrong

To use any right, email us at the address below.

8.Cookies

Our web dashboard uses essential cookies (for secure login) and optional analytics cookies. You can manage them through Cookie Settings.

9.Children

MoneyMapper is for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

10.Data Breach

If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

11.Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy.

Major changes will be shared by email or in-app notice.
