Grievance Redressal Policy

How to raise a complaint, and how we resolve it

Effective Date: 28 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 15 October 2026  |  Applies to: All users of the MoneyMapper website and web application.

MoneyMapper (operated by Monezra Mapper Private Limited) is committed to addressing user complaints and grievances — whether relating to account/billing issues, data privacy under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, service quality, or content on the Platform — in a fair, transparent, and timely manner, in line with applicable Indian consumer protection and data protection law.

2.What You Can Raise a Grievance About

  • Billing, subscription, or payment/auto-debit issues;
  • Concerns about how your personal or financial data has been collected, used, or stored;
  • Requests to access, correct, or delete your personal data (Data Principal rights under the DPDPA);
  • Complaints regarding content, calculators, or the Financial Fitness Score;
  • Account security concerns, including suspected unauthorized access;
  • Any other concern regarding your experience on the Platform.

3.How to Raise a Grievance

  1. Email the Grievance Officer at grievance@moneymapper.in, with a subject line that clearly describes the issue (e.g., "Billing Complaint", "Data Deletion Request").
  2. Include your registered email ID/mobile number, a description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots or transaction reference numbers.
  3. Alternatively, you may use the in-app "Help & Support" / "Raise a Complaint" option, where available.
  4. You may also write to us at the correspondence address given in Section 1.

There is no fee for raising a grievance. Every complaint receives a unique reference number, which you can quote in all further communication.

4.Resolution Process & Timelines

StepActionTimeline
1. AcknowledgementGrievance Officer acknowledges receipt of your complaintWithin 48 hours of receipt
2. InvestigationRelevant team investigates the complaint (billing, tech, data privacy, as applicable)Within 7 working days
3. ResolutionFinal resolution communicated to the user, including reasoningWithin 15 working days from date of receipt (or such other period as may be required under applicable law, e.g., DPDPA timelines for data-related requests)
4. EscalationIf unresolved, user may request escalation to senior managementEscalation reviewed within a further 7 working days

For grievances specifically relating to personal data processing (as a Data Principal under the DPDPA), we will respond within the timeline prescribed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

5.Confidentiality

All grievances and the personal information shared in them are treated as confidential and used only to investigate and resolve your complaint, in line with our Privacy Policy. No user will face any adverse action or loss of service for raising a genuine grievance.

6.Escalation — Data Protection Board of India

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer regarding a personal data grievance, you have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India, constituted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, in the manner prescribed by the Board.

7.Escalation — Consumer Forums

For grievances relating to deficiency in service or unfair trade practice, you may also approach the appropriate consumer dispute redressal forum/commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or contact the National Consumer Helpline at 1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in.

8.Review of this Policy

This Grievance Redressal Policy will be reviewed periodically and updated as required by law or business need. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last Updated" date shown at the top.
