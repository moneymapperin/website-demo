Cookie Policy

For the MoneyMapper web application and website login dashboard

Effective Date: 28 August 2026  |  Applies to: moneymapper.in and the web-based login dashboard/application.

This Cookie Policy explains how MoneyMapper (Monezra Mapper Private Limited) uses cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies on our website and web application, including for session management, authentication, and analytics purposes on the web login dashboard.

1.What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser/device when you visit a website or use a web application. They help the website remember your actions and preferences (such as staying logged in) and help us understand how the Platform is used.

2.Categories of Cookies We Use

CategoryPurposeExamplesCan You Disable?
Essential Required for core functionality of the web login dashboard — without these, you cannot log in or use the Platform securely. Session identifier cookie, authentication/JWT token, CSRF-protection token, load-balancer routing cookie No — essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the Platform to function and to keep your account secure.
Functional Remember your preferences and settings across sessions. Selected currency/display preferences, "remember this device" preference, last-viewed pillar/dashboard tab Yes — via cookie settings; disabling may reduce convenience.
Analytical / Performance Help us understand how users navigate the Platform, which features are used, and where users face friction, so we can improve the Financial Fitness Score experience. Page-view tracking, click/event tracking, session-duration tracking, aggregated funnel analytics (e.g., signup-to-subscription conversion) Yes — you may opt out via the cookie consent banner/settings.

3.Authentication Tokens & Session Management

  • When you log in to the web dashboard, we issue a session cookie and/or authentication token (e.g., JWT) to keep you securely logged in without requiring you to re-enter your password on every page.
  • These tokens are set with security attributes (such as HttpOnly, Secure, and appropriate SameSite settings) to reduce the risk of theft via cross-site scripting or cross-site request forgery.
  • Session cookies/tokens automatically expire after a period of inactivity or upon logout, in accordance with our account security practices described in our Terms and Conditions.
  • If you log in from multiple devices, a separate session token is generated per device/browser, in line with our multi-device login rules.

4.Third-Party Cookies

We may use reputable third-party analytics providers (e.g., web/product analytics tools) that set their own cookies to help us measure Platform usage in aggregate. These providers are contractually bound to process data only for the purposes we specify and in compliance with applicable Indian data protection law.

5.Managing Your Cookie Preferences

  • On your first visit, you will see a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept all cookies, or to customize/reject non-essential (functional and analytical) cookies.
  • You can change your cookie preferences at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the website footer or app settings.
  • You can also manage or delete cookies directly through your browser settings; note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in or using the Platform.

6.Relationship to Our Privacy Policy

Any personal data collected through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy, including our commitment to storing data on servers located within India.

7.Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. Please check this page periodically.

8.Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, contact info@moneymapper.in. For complaints, see our Grievance Redressal Policy.
